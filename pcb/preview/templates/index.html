Open full screen. Concept case: every part is on the board's inner side, so the blank face sits flat against the case front. The 2 mics listen through holes in the board straight out of the front, and the RGB LED shines out through a slot in the board, so no light pipe is needed. The USB-C port opens through the case side wall. The 150 mAh round cell and the coin motor sit behind the parts. Parts without a vendor 3D model are drawn as simple blocks.
